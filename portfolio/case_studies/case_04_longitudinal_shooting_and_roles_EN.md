# Case Study 04: Longitudinal Shooting Evolution & Functional Player Archetypes

## 1. The Challenge
Understanding how international basketball has evolved across two decades (2005–2024) in 3-point volume, pace, and player tactical roles.

## 2. Key Findings
- **3PAr Surge**: 3-point attempt rate grew from ~28% in 2005 to over 39% in 2024.
- **Pace Dynamics**: Tournament pace stabilized around 72 possessions per 40 minutes with increased transition efficiency.
- **Functional Clustering**: Discovered 6 stable player archetypes across the 20-year window.
