# Case Study 01: Tactical Decision Support & Pre-Game Coaching Briefs

## 1. The Challenge
Coaching staffs operating in international tournament play have limited preparation windows (often <24 hours between games) and face information overload from raw box scores.

## 2. The Analytical Solution
We engineered an automated 1.5-page pre-game scouting brief synthesizing:
- Four Factors matchup differentials adjusted for tournament pace.
- Individual player shot quality distribution and usage roles.
- Key tactical contradictions between quantitative efficiency and historical tendencies.

## 3. Real-World Impact
Enables coaching staffs to focus team preparation on high-leverage tactical advantages while eliminating statistical noise.
