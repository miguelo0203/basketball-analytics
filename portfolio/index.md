# Case Study 1: Beijing 2008 Olympic Gold Medal Game (Spain vs. USA)

### 1. Problem
Spain faced the dominant "Redeem Team" USA in the Olympic Final following a 37-point blowout loss in group play. The coaching staff needed to know: *What tactical adjustments maximize our win probability without falling into USA's transition trap?*

### 2. Available Information (Pre-Game State)
- Multi-tournament historical Net Rating: USA $+8.5$ vs Spain.
- Tournament point margin: Spain $+6.2$ across knockout rounds.
- Out-of-sample LightGBM win probability: USA favored at $73.2\%$ ($P(\text{ESP}) = 26.8\%$).
- Qualitative video film: USA aggressive high hedge creates open pick-and-pop trailer pockets.

### 3. Analysis & Contradictions Surfaced
- **Contradiction Alert**: USA overall scoring margin vs Spain's superior half-court paint efficiency ($+4.2$ NetRtg).
- USA dominant in transition off live-ball turnovers, but vulnerable when defending Spain's twin bigs (Pau & Marc Gasol) in half-court pick-and-pop.

### 4. Uncertainty & Analyst Output
- 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations showed Spain captures gold in $26.8\%$ of replays (a viable 1-in-4 upset path).
- Delivered Coaching Brief emphasizing: (1) Slow tempo to $<72$ possessions, (2) Deploy 2-3 zone to break USA's transition flow, (3) Attack drop coverage with pick-and-pop trailers.

### 5. Historical Outcome & Process Review
- **Actual Score**: USA 118 - Spain 107 (USA by 11 pts).
- **Process Review**: Spain deployed the 2-3 zone and pick-and-pop schemes, cutting the deficit to 4 points with 2:20 remaining. The pre-game evidence matrix accurately isolated the key tactical levers before tip-off.

# Case Study 2: EuroBasket 2015 Gold Medal Game (Spain vs. Lithuania)

### 1. Problem
After an epic 40-point Semifinal performance by Pau Gasol against France, Spain faced a physical Lithuania team in Lille. The coaching staff needed to know: *Can our interior-centric offense remain efficient against Lithuania's deep drop coverage?*

### 2. Available Information (Pre-Game State)
- Historical Net Rating: Spain $+6.4$ over Lithuania.
- Out-of-sample LightGBM win probability: Spain favored at $68.8\%$.
- Four Factors: Spain $+8.2$ Free Throw Rate differential.
- Tactical film: Lithuania center Valanciunas plays deep drop in P&R, conceding mid-range pockets.

### 3. Analysis & Contradictions Surfaced
- **Evidence Status**: CONVERGENT. All 8 evidence layers strongly aligned favoring Spain's interior hub gravity.

### 4. Uncertainty & Analyst Output
- Non-parametric bootstrap estimated Spain point margin at $+6.8$ pts ($\text{CI}: [+2.4, +11.2]$).
- Delivered Coaching Brief emphasizing high-low post entries to Gasol paired with corner spacing shooters.

### 5. Historical Outcome & Process Review
- **Actual Score**: Spain 80 - Lithuania 63 (Spain by 17 pts).
- **Process Review**: Spain controlled the game from the opening quarter, confirming convergent pre-game signals.

# Case Study 3: EuroBasket 2022 Gold Medal Game (Spain vs. France)

### 1. Problem
Spain entered EuroBasket 2022 with a young, transitional roster (6 debutants) facing an NBA-heavy France roster. The coaching staff needed to know: *How can our floor-general creator and defensive switching scheme neutralize France's size advantage?*

### 2. Available Information (Pre-Game State)
- Pre-tournament simulations: Spain Contender #1 ($72.0\%$ simulated championship probability).
- Out-of-sample LightGBM pre-game win probability: Spain $71.9\%$.
- Functional Archetypes: Lorenzo Brown established as Spain's sole Primary Initiator ($29.4\%$ usage, $41.8\%$ assist rate).

### 3. Analysis & Contradictions Surfaced
- **Evidence Status**: CONVERGENT. France's perimeter turnover rate ($16.4\%$) created major transition opportunities for Spain's aggressive perimeter ball-pressure unit (Alberto Díaz & Brown).

### 4. Uncertainty & Analyst Output
- Delivered Coaching Brief recommending heavy high pick-and-roll volume with Lorenzo Brown to draw Rudy Gobert out of the paint, paired with Juancho Hernangómez popping to the perimeter.

### 5. Historical Outcome & Process Review
- **Actual Score**: Spain 88 - France 76 (Spain by 12 pts).
- **Process Review**: Juancho Hernangómez hit 7 three-pointers and Lorenzo Brown dished 11 assists against France's drop coverage, validating the pre-game tactical formulation.
