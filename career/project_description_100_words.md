### Copy-Paste Text (98 Words)

An end-to-end historical basketball analytics and decision-support system covering 20 years of international competitions (18 tournaments, 1,145 games, 27,353 player-games). Powered by an immutable DuckDB relational warehouse and Python feature pipelines, the system implements 17-fold chronological walk-forward machine learning (LightGBM, ECE = 0.0314), functional player role taxonomies, and double-coded tactical video film (Cohen's Kappa = 0.80). An interactive Streamlit workspace generates concise pre-game coaching briefs, surfaces statistical vs. film contradictions, and isolates pre-game information states to eliminate hindsight bias, demonstrating how data analysts deliver actionable, uncertainty-aware evidence to coaching staffs and sporting directors.
