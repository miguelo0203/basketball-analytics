# 04 — Basketball & Coaching Tactical Questions & Answers
## Four Factors, Pick-and-Roll Schemes & Spacing

---

### Q1: How do you translate Dean Oliver's Four Factors into tactical coaching concepts?
**Answer**:
> *"I translate raw statistics into possession realities:
> - **Effective Field Goal % (eFG%)** $\rightarrow$ Shot selection quality and interior finishing.
> - **Turnover Rate (TOV%)** $\rightarrow$ Ball security against point-of-attack pressure and transition prevention.
> - **Offensive Rebound % (ORB%)** $\rightarrow$ Second-chance creation vs. transition retreat discipline.
> - **Free Throw Rate (FTR)** $\rightarrow$ Downhill rim attack and drawing contact against dropping bigs."*

---

### Q2: How do you evaluate pick-and-roll drop coverage versus aggressive hedge schemes?
**Answer**:
> *"When a defense drops their big below the dotted line, they protect the restricted area but concede mid-range pullups and pick-and-pop perimeter trailer pockets. When a defense plays an aggressive high hedge or blitz, they force the ball out of the creator's hands but expose the weakside paint to 4-on-3 short-roll passes and corner skip shots."*

---

### Q3: How do you evaluate a low-boxscore defensive anchor?
**Answer**:
> *"Traditional boxscores miss point-of-attack screen navigation and vertical rim deterrence. In our system, we evaluate functional role archetypes via Net Rating impact, opponent turnover generation, and qualitative video coding on closeout contest speeds ($\kappa = 0.80$)."*
