# 07 — Strategic Questions to Ask the Hiring Employer
## High-Impact Questions for a Junior Basketball Analyst to Ask Coaching & Analytics Leadership

---

### Category 1: Tactical & Coaching Staff Integration
1. *"How does the coaching staff currently consume analytical information during a standard game-week (e.g. printed 2-page briefs, video coordinator clips, or post-practice debriefs)?"*
2. *"How are tactical disagreements between video scouting and statistical metrics currently resolved inside the coaching room?"*
3. *"What is the expected turnaround time from the final buzzer of a match to the delivery of the post-game process review?"*

---

### Category 2: Data Infrastructure & Departmental Workflows
4. *"What raw data feeds does the organization currently license (e.g. Second Spectrum optical tracking, Synergy Sports play-by-play, Catapult GPS physical load telemetry)?"*
5. *"How much of the department's weekly reporting is automated via SQL/Python pipelines versus manually compiled in spreadsheets?"*
6. *"What database infrastructure and cloud tooling (e.g. PostgreSQL, DuckDB, Snowflake, AWS/GCP) does the team currently use?"*

---

### Category 3: Organizational Success & Career Development
7. *"What would a successful first 90 days look like for a new analyst joining your department?"*
8. *"How does the analytics department balance immediate short-term opponent preparation with long-term strategic roster planning and draft/scouting research?"*
