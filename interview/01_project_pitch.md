# 01 — Project Pitch: 60 Seconds & 2 Minutes
## The Elevator Pitch for Basketball Data Analyst Positions

---

### The 60-Second Version
> *"I am a basketball data analyst who builds end-to-end analytical pipelines that turn noisy sports data into actionable coaching decision support. I built a comprehensive historical analytics system covering 20 years of international basketball—18 tournaments, 1,145 games, and over 27,000 player performances. The system integrates DuckDB relational data engineering, functional player archetypes, double-coded video film ($\kappa = 0.80$), expanding 17-fold walk-forward machine learning ($\text{ECE} = 0.0314$), and 180,000 Monte Carlo simulations. The system powers an interactive Analyst Workspace that isolates pre-game information to eliminate hindsight bias and automatically surfaces tactical contradictions between stats and video. My philosophy is that the analyst's role is not to replace the head coach, but to give decision-makers cleaner evidence, deeper context, and calibrated uncertainty to make better basketball decisions."*

---

### The 2-Minute Version
> *"When looking at modern sports analytics, the biggest gap is often between complex data science models and actual on-court coaching decisions. In high-pressure tournaments, raw boxscore stats fluctuate wildly because of small-sample shooting noise, and coaching staffs are susceptible to recency bias.
>
> To address this, I built an end-to-end decision-support system covering 20 years of senior men's international basketball. I built the entire stack from scratch: an immutable DuckDB relational warehouse with SHA-256 validation, longitudinal econometric modeling on FIBA rule changes, K-Means++ clustering on 3,767 qualified player campaigns to discover functional on-court roles, and double-coded video film analysis.
>
> For predictive modeling, I used 17 expanding chronological walk-forward folds so future data never leaks into past predictions. I calibrated the models to an Expected Calibration Error of 0.0314 and propagated probabilities through 180,000 Monte Carlo tournament simulations.
>
> But the core of the project is the operational Analyst Workspace: it generates concise, 1.5-page pre-game coaching briefs, surfaces hidden contradictions between statistical models and video film, and enforces a strict anti-hindsight barrier. If I joined your organization, I would bring this exact engineering rigor, basketball domain fluency, and operational humility to support your coaching staff every game."*
