From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Group F of the EuroBasket 2013 took place between 12 and 16 September 2013. The group played all of its games at Arena Stožice in Ljubljana, Slovenia.

The group composed of the best three teams from Group C and D. The four best ranked teams advanced to the knockout stage.
