From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Group C of EuroBasket 2017 consisted of Croatia, Czech Republic, Hungary, Montenegro, Romania and Spain. The games were played between 1 and 7 September 2017. All games were played at the Polyvalent Hall in Cluj-Napoca, Romania.[1]
