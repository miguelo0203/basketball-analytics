# 1. The 12-Step Professional Analyst Workflow

```text
1. DEFINE THE QUESTION        ── Identify the specific coaching or sporting decision.
2. ESTABLISH CONTEXT          ── Inspect tournament stage, opponent strength, rest days.
3. CHECK DATA QUALITY         ── Verify boxscore provenance and zero temporal leakage.
4. REVIEW QUANTITATIVE SIGNAL ── Analyze historical Net Rating and Four Factors metrics.
5. REVIEW TACTICAL EVIDENCE   ── Examine double-coded video film on P&R and coverage schemes.
6. CHECK MODEL OUTPUT         ── Query calibrated out-of-sample win probability (ECE = 0.0314).
7. CHECK UNCERTAINTY          ── Inspect Clustered Bootstrap 95% CIs and sample exposure tiers.
8. IDENTIFY CONTRADICTIONS    ── Audit discrepancies between quantitative models and film.
9. FORMULATE QUESTIONS        ── Draft concrete, actionable questions for coaching staff.
10. DELIVER COACHING BRIEF    ── Hand off concise, structured brief prior to tip-off.
11. COACH MAKES DECISION      ── Human coaching staff exercises tactical authority.
12. POST-GAME PROCESS REVIEW  ── Audit deviations against pre-game expectations.
```

# 3. Post-Game Process Review Methodology

A professional analyst is evaluated on **process quality and calibration**, not outcome bias:
- **Directional Alignment**: Did the pre-game favorite win?
- **Uncertainty Bounds**: Was the final point margin within the model's 95% bootstrap spread?
- **Contradiction Evaluation**: Did the pre-game contradiction alert explain unexpected tactical game flows?
- **Process Scorecard**: Isolates random variance (e.g. 26% 3-point shooting night) from systematic tactical flaws.
