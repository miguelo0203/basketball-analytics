# MVP-4 Recruitment Briefs & Tactical Specifications
## Professional Decision-Support Framework (2005–2025)

---

## 1. Case A: Secondary Creation Wing

- **Tactical Rationale**: The team possesses a dominant interior big and a ball-dominant point guard, but requires a secondary perimeter creator who can attack closeouts, space the floor, and disrupt passing lanes without demanding high offensive usage.
- **Target Role Archetypes**: `Two-Way Scoring Wing / Slasher` or `Perimeter Movement Shooter / Spacer`
- **Age Window**: $20\text{--}32\text{ years}$
- **Height Requirement**: $\ge 192\text{ cm}$
- **Mandatory Quantitative Thresholds**:
  - $3\text{PAr} \ge 0.35$ (Demonstrated willingness to space from distance)
  - $TS\% \ge 0.50$ (Acceptable baseline scoring efficiency)
  - $AST\% \ge 0.10$ (Secondary passing creation)
  - $STL/40 \ge 0.8$ (Point-of-attack / passing lane disruption)
  - $MIN \ge 80.0$ (Minimum tournament rotation exposure)
- **Preferred Quantitative Target**: $3\text{PAr} \ge 0.45, TS\% \ge 0.55, AST\% \ge 0.15, STL/40 \ge 1.2$
- **Dimensional Weights**: Perimeter ($1.2$), Creation ($1.2$), Efficiency ($1.1$), Defense ($1.1$), Scoring ($0.8$)
- **Disqualifying Conditions**: $TOV\% \ge 0.20$, $3\text{PAr} < 0.25$, $MIN < 60$.

---

## 2. Case B: Defensive / Spacing Guard

- **Tactical Rationale**: The roster requires a low-maintenance off-ball guard to pair with an elite playmaker. Must hit open catch-and-shoot 3s, execute point-of-attack defensive ball pressure, and operate with zero turnover friction.
- **Target Role Archetypes**: `Perimeter Movement Shooter / Spacer` or `Primary Initiator / Floor General`
- **Age Window**: $20\text{--}32\text{ years}$
- **Height Requirement**: $\ge 180\text{ cm}$
- **Mandatory Quantitative Thresholds**:
  - $3\text{PAr} \ge 0.45$ (Heavy perimeter orientation)
  - $TS\% \ge 0.52$ (Above-average true shooting efficiency)
  - $STL/40 \ge 1.0$ (Aggressive on-ball disruption)
  - $TOV\% \le 0.16$ (Low ball-handling turnover penalty)
  - $MIN \ge 80.0$
- **Preferred Quantitative Target**: $3\text{PAr} \ge 0.55, TS\% \ge 0.58, STL/40 \ge 1.5$
- **Dimensional Weights**: Perimeter ($1.5$), Efficiency ($1.3$), Defense ($1.3$), Creation ($0.6$), Scoring ($0.8$)
- **Disqualifying Conditions**: $3\text{PAr} < 0.35$, $TS\% < 0.50$, $STL/40 < 0.6$.

---

## 3. Case C: Stretch / Connector Forward

- **Tactical Rationale**: Modern frontcourt anchor capable of pulling opposing rim protectors out of the paint via pick-and-pop spacing while contributing on the defensive glass and moving the ball to the weak side.
- **Target Role Archetypes**: `Stretch Big / Pick-and-Pop Forward` or `Low-Block Anchor / Interior Scorer`
- **Age Window**: $21\text{--}34\text{ years}$
- **Height Requirement**: $\ge 202\text{ cm}$
- **Mandatory Quantitative Thresholds**:
  - $3\text{PAr} \ge 0.25$ (Floor-spacing capability for a frontcourt player)
  - $TS\% \ge 0.52$ (Solid finishing and shooting efficiency)
  - $\text{Total REB\%} \ge 0.15$ (Two-way glass presence)
  - $MIN \ge 80.0$
- **Preferred Quantitative Target**: $3\text{PAr} \ge 0.35, TS\% \ge 0.58, \text{Total REB\%} \ge 0.22, AST\% \ge 0.08$
- **Dimensional Weights**: Perimeter ($1.3$), Rebounding ($1.3$), Efficiency ($1.2$), Defense ($0.8$), Scoring ($0.9$)
- **Disqualifying Conditions**: $3\text{PAr} < 0.15$, $TS\% < 0.48$, $\text{Total REB\%} < 0.10$.
