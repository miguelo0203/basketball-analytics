# The 60-Second Elevator Pitch
## International Basketball Historical Analytics (2005–2025)

**Status**: Formally Certified Elevator Pitch  
**Target Duration**: 60 Seconds (150 Words)  

---

### Elevator Pitch Script:

> *"I am a basketball data analyst who builds end-to-end analytical systems that translate complex sports data into actionable coaching decision support.*
>
> *I built a comprehensive analytical pipeline covering 20 years of international senior men's basketball—18 tournaments, 1,145 games, and over 27,000 player performances. The system integrates deterministic DuckDB data engineering, functional player archetypes, double-coded tactical video film, expanding 17-fold walk-forward machine learning, and 180,000 Monte Carlo tournament simulations.*
>
> *Most importantly, the system powers an interactive Analyst Decision Workspace that generates pre-game Coaching Briefs. It isolates pre-game information to eliminate hindsight bias, explicitly surfaces tactical contradictions between stats and video film, and quantifies uncertainty.*
>
> *My philosophy is simple: the role of the analyst is not to replace the coach or make the decision. The role of the analyst is to give decision-makers cleaner evidence, deeper context, and calibrated uncertainty to make better basketball decisions."*
