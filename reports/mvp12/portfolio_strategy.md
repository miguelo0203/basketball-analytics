# 2. The Four Core Communication Pillars

1. **Evidence over Prediction**: We do not claim to forecast the future with certainty; we provide calibrated probabilities ($ECE = 0.0314$) and non-parametric variance intervals.
2. **Tactical-Quantitative Synergy**: Boxscores and metrics establish baseline priors; double-coded video film ($\kappa = 0.80$) reveals scheme execution and contextual nuance.
3. **Contradiction Transparency**: When statistical models and film diverge, we surface the conflict immediately as a coaching inquiry rather than hiding discrepancies.
4. **Human Decision Ownership**: The analyst's job is to clarify uncertainty and structure evidence; the Head Coach and Sporting Director make the final basketball decisions.
