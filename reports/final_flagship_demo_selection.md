# 2. Selected Flagship Demonstration: Beijing 2008 Olympic Final (Spain vs. USA)

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SELECTED FLAGSHIP: BEIJING 2008 OLYMPIC GOLD MEDAL GAME (SPAIN VS. USA)                            |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Why Beijing 2008 is the Single Strongest Demonstration:

1. **Maximum Basketball Relevance**: Everyone in basketball knows the 2008 USA "Redeem Team" and Spain's golden generation. It requires zero setup time to explain who the teams were.
2. **Sharp Contradiction Detection**: It demonstrates the core value of the analyst:
   - *Statistical Prior*: USA heavily favored ($73.2\%$).
   - *Film & Four Factors Nuance*: USA dominant in fast-break transition off live turnovers, but Spain possessed a $+4.2$ Net Rating advantage in half-court paint efficiency and USA was vulnerable in P&R drop coverage against pick-and-pop trailers.
3. **Actionable Coaching Inquiries**: The brief formulates two concrete, non-prescriptive tactical questions:
   - *Can we deploy a 2-3 zone to break USA's transition rhythm?*
   - *Can Pau and Marc Gasol punish USA's drop coverage by trailing to the 3-point line?*
4. **Anti-Hindsight Outcome & Process Review**: In reality, Spain executed those exact adjustments and cut the deficit to 4 points with 2 minutes left in a historic 118–107 battle, proving that the pre-game analysis identified the true underlying basketball dynamics.
5. **Fast & Interactive**: Can be demonstrated smoothly in the Streamlit workspace in under 6 minutes (`src/analytics/mvp10_analyst_workspace.py`).
