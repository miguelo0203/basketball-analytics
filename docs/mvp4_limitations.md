# MVP-4 Methodological Limitations & Decision-Support Caveats
## International Basketball Historical Analytics (2005–2025)

--------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT INTERNATIONAL ANALYTICS CANNOT MEASURE
--------------------------------------------------

1. **Domestic Club Context vs. National Team Context**:
   - International tournament play represents high-leverage, short-duration tournament basketball ($5\text{--}11$ games over 2–3 weeks).
   - A player's role in a national team (e.g. secondary spot-up shooter) may differ significantly from their 82-game domestic club responsibility.

2. **Absence of Medical and Biomechanical Data**:
   - Quantitative boxscore analytics cannot observe joint health, injury history, fatigue recovery profiles, or structural biomechanics.

3. **Psychological, Intangible, and Cultural Factors**:
   - Locker room presence, leadership, tactical coachability, language adaptability, and communication cannot be extracted from boxscore data.

--------------------------------------------------
2. SAMPLE-SIZE LIMITS & ESTIMATION VOLATILITY
--------------------------------------------------

1. **Short Tournament Variance**:
   - 3-point shooting percentages over $30\text{--}50$ tournament attempts possess high random noise. We emphasize 3P Attempt Rate ($3\text{PAr}$) because shot selection volume stabilizes faster than shooting percentage.
2. **Opponent Group Quality Differences**:
   - Preliminary round blowouts against lower-ranked federations can inflate volume numbers. The multi-stage reliability filter and competition-level context normalization help mitigate this distortion.

--------------------------------------------------
3. THE STRICT ANALYTICAL BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------

Quantitative decision support identifies **WHAT** a candidate produced in historical games. It must never replace the human scouting and coaching evaluation that observes **HOW** and **WHY** on tape.
